## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for Kenyan secondary school students seeking peer support, mental health literacy, and guidance in an environment free of clinical judgment or stigma. The brand personality balances peer-to-peer warmth, vulnerability, cultural familiarity, and optimism. It feels like confiding in a trusted, mature friend under the shade of an acacia tree rather than entering a cold hospital ward.

The visual direction merges **Warm Editorial Warmth** with **Humanist Minimalism**:
- **Non-Clinical Approach**: Avoids sterile whites, alarmist reds, and intimidating medical jargon. Replaces diagnostic motifs with expressive, uplifting, and relatable storytelling components.
- **Cultural Resonance & Tactility**: Leverages earthy undertones reminiscent of sun-warmed clay, organic greens, and textured paper warmth to cultivate a grounded sanctuary.
- **Supportive Conversational UI**: Interactive prompts, journaling spaces, and peer-matching flows prioritize gentle encouragement, micro-affirmations, and clear, low-pressure cognitive load.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in calming African earth tones, balancing emotional decompression with purposeful engagement:

- **Primary (`#3A6351` - Kipre Sage / Deep Eucalyptus)**: A comforting, grounded foliage green serving as the anchor for key interactive targets, deep focus zones, and validation states. It promotes calmness and safety.
- **Secondary (`#C86D51` - Warm Terracotta)**: A rich, humanizing clay red-orange used for supportive callouts, highlights, peer-connection badges, and conversational emphasis without creating urgency or anxiety.
- **Tertiary (`#DDA74F` - Savannah Amber)**: An uplifting sunburst tone dedicated to micro-achievements, emotional check-in streaks, gentle warnings, and mood reflection accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F9F6F0` - Warm Alabaster / Cream)**: An organic, soft-tinted backdrop designed to minimize screen glare, alleviate eye strain, and completely eliminate hospital-like clinical white.
- **Text & Structure (`#25302B` - Deep Forest Slate)**: High-contrast, deeply saturated dark slate providing exceptional readability while maintaining a natural, organic temperance.

## Typography

The type pairing merges the structured, modern editorial character of **Epilogue** with the friendly, accessible rhythm of **Plus Jakarta Sans**:

- **Epilogue** provides distinct, warm headings with an intentional human signature, anchoring article covers, reflective questions, and conversational milestones without feeling rigid.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers optimal legibility for adolescent reading on varying screen qualities and handheld devices across Kenya. Its open apertures and soft curves cultivate comfort during long narrative reads and diary interactions.
- All copy should use generous line-heights to support students dealing with stress, cognitive fatigue, or high cognitive load.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy implements a **Fluid Adaptive Grid** with generous breathing room to foster an unhurried, pressure-free user flow:

- **Mobile Viewport (< 640px)**: 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) outer margins and gutters. Single-column card stacks prevent cognitive fragmentation and support comfortable one-handed thumb interaction.
- **Tablet Viewport (640px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 32px (`2rem`) outer margins and 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters. Accommodates split views for mood tracking and reading feeds.
- **Desktop Viewport (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1120px` to maintain focused, readable eye spans. Side rails house gentle self-care toolbars and emergency contact shortcuts.
- **Whitespace Discipline**: Avoid dense information clustering. Interactive choices (such as mood trackers or feeling tags) must have generous tap zones separated by at least `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through **Tonal Layering** and **Soft Ambient Shadows**, deliberately eschewing artificial, harsh drop shadows or sterile clinic dividers:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base canvas rests at `#F9F6F0`. Raised elements, interactive cards, and bottom sheets adopt pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale sage wash (`#EDF2EE`) to define physical presence through natural contrast.
- **Ambient Shadow Character**: When elevation is required (such as floating help pills, anonymous chat bars, or modal sheets), shadows use an extra-diffused blur tinted with natural earth tones: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(37, 48, 43, 0.08)`.
- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines**: Cards and inputs utilize subtle boundary strokes (`1px solid rgba(58, 99, 81, 0.12)`) in place of harsh dark borders, keeping surfaces distinct without creating visual clutter.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry to reinforce softness, security, and an approachable physical demeanor:

- **Standard Base Components**: Buttons, inputs, chips, and small prompt bubbles adopt `0.5rem` (8px) corner radii.
- **Container Surfaces (`rounded-lg`)**: Content cards, dialogue panels, peer reflection containers, and notification banners use `1rem` (16px) corners.
- **Featured & Sanctuary Surfaces (`rounded-xl`)**: Emergency helpline cards, mindfulness audio cards, and modal bottom sheets utilize `1.5rem` (24px) corners for a protective, nested feel.
- **Interactive Badges & Avatars**: Emotion check-in buttons and anonymous peer badges leverage full pill shapes (`9999px`) to maintain tactile playfulness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid eucalyptus (`#3A6351`) with white text and `rounded-md` (8px) geometry. Generous padding (`12px 24px`) with bold, clear typography.
- **Secondary / Expressive**: Warm terracotta (`#C86D51`) or subtle tinted wash (`#F0E5DE`) for optional sharing, creative journaling, and non-mandatory next steps.
- **Crisis / Direct Help Button**: Always visible floating pill with amber-tinted badge treatment; clear, friendly copy such as *"Talk to someone now"* rather than sterile alert cues.

### Chips & Emotion Selectors
- Pill-shaped tags used for self-reporting feelings (e.g., *Stressed*, *Hopeful*, *Exams*, *Family*).
- Default: `#F9F6F0` background with a subtle border (`rgba(58, 99, 81, 0.16)`) and `#25302B` label.
- Selected: Deep eucalyptus (`#3A6351`) fill with white text, accompanied by a soft spring scale animation on tap.

### Input Fields & Reflection Prompts
- Large, comfortable inputs framed by warm neutral backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`) and a soft outline (`#D8DFD9`).
- Active focus state transitions smoothly to a 2px primary eucalyptus border with a delicate ambient ring (`rgba(58, 99, 81, 0.15)`).
- Journaling textareas feature friendly placeholder prompts and counter text framed in casual, encouraging language.

### Cards & Peer Stories
- Built on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with `1rem` rounded corners and subtle ghost borders (`1px solid rgba(58, 99, 81, 0.08)`).
- Incorporates category accents using top-border color pinstripes (terracotta for peer stories, eucalyptus for wellness tools, amber for student advice).
- Elevated interactive cards feature subtle lift behavior without stark scale shifts.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Rounded check indicators (minimum 20px target area) in `#3A6351` with organic checkmark icons.
- Avoids rigid square geometries; uses soft organic contours (`4px` radii for checkboxes, fully circular for radios) with supportive micro-copy alongside each choice.

### Specialized Conversational Patterns
- **Anonymous Mood Check-in Barometer**: A daily one-tap sliding or card-based check-in using illustrated organic shapes representing mood states.
- **Safe Exit & Privacy Mode Banner**: A non-intrusive floating pill allowing students in shared study halls or homes to quickly disguise or mute screen content with a single tap.
